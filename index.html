Free Fire mein headshot rate badhane ke 6 aasaan tips

Gaming tips · 4 min read

Headshot hi Free Fire ka asli game changer hai. Achhi baat ye hai ki iske liye koi hack nahi, sirf sahi settings aur practice chahiye. Ye 6 cheezein roz follow karo, farak khud dikhega.

1. Sensitivity apne phone ke hisaab se set karo

Har phone alag hota hai, isliye kisi aur ki sensitivity copy karne se kaam nahi chalta. Training ground mein general aur red dot sensitivity thodi thodi badha kar test karo, jahan crosshair head tak smooth jaaye wahi rakho.

2. Crosshair hamesha head level par rakho

Chalte waqt bhi crosshair ko zameen par mat chhodo. Jab enemy saamne aaye to sirf thoda sa adjust karna pade, yahi one-tap headshot ka raaz hai.

3. Drag shot ki practice roz 10 minute

Fire button dabate hi halka upar drag karo. Shuru mein bullets upar jayengi, lekin 4-5 din ki practice ke baad haath set ho jaata hai.

4. Sahi gun chuno

Close range ke liye M1887 ya MP40, mid range ke liye AR jaise SCAR ya Woodpecker. Har gun ka recoil alag hai, ek hi gun par mastery pehle banao.

5. HUD layout comfortable banao

  • Fire button wahan rakho jahan angootha naturally jaata hai
  • Scope aur crouch button paas rakho
  • Zarurat ho to 3 ya 4 finger claw try karo

6. Apne gameplay ki recording dekho

Har match ke baad 2 minute apni recording dekho. Kahan bullet miss hui, kahan cover nahi liya, ye galtiyan dekh kar sabse jaldi improvement hota hai.

Bas itna hi! Practice regular rakho, aur agle step par aur kaam ki baatein milengi.
